This mod adds different kinds of sea life to the game.  There are no interactions with the animals as they are intended for ambience, so no you can't go whaling in this mod.

Requires BepInEx5.
* Install to your BepInEx plugins folder.

# Features:
* Spawns animals around your boat.
* Removes animals which get too far away from you so you don't have whales doing their thing in Aestrin while you're visiting Fire Fish Lagoon.
* Collision avoidance.
* Animals spawn in at random sizes.
* Random behaviors, such as surfacing and breaching.
* Particle effects where appropriate.
* Animals follow the height of the waves.

# Known issues:
* Collisions have been disabled on the whales for now until I can come up with some better ship-to-whale impact logic. For now they just do their best to avoid hitting your ship and other objects.

# Current animals:
* Fin Whale, a 26 meter baleen whale.
* Orca, a large porpoise which can group into pods and may take an interest in ships.

I'll add more animals as I have the time and motivation for.
